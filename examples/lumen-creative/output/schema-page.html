Money

Payments

Payments

One record = one incoming payment (or an expected one). Refunds are entered here too, with Kind = Refund.

Who fills it: Whoever issues invoices; status changes to Paid when the money arrives

linked to: Accounts, Clients, Retainers

Fields · 16
  • Paymentnumber (auto)
    Assigned automatically.
  • Client→ Clientsrequired
  • Amountmoneyrequired
    Positive number, even for refunds — the Kind field decides the sign.
  • Kindsingle choicerequired
    Internal transfer = money moved between your own accounts. It is not revenue.
    Client payment · Refund · Other income · Internal transfer
  • Statussingle choicerequired
    Expected · Invoiced · Paid · Cancelled
  • Expected datedate
  • Payment datedaterequired
    The day the money arrived. Without it the payment falls out of every month.
  • Invoice numbertext
  • Account→ Accounts
    Where the money landed.
  • Commentlong text
  • Monthformulacomputed
    Month of payment, for reports. Computed.
  • Revenue amountformulacomputed
    What counts as revenue: paid, not a transfer, refunds negative. Computed — do not edit.
  • Cash informulacomputed
    Effect on the account balance (transfers included). Computed.
  • Checkformulacomputed
    Shows a warning when something important is missing.
  • Retainer→ Retainers
  • Periodtext
    Which month the payment covers, as YYYY-MM. Lets you see a missed month.
